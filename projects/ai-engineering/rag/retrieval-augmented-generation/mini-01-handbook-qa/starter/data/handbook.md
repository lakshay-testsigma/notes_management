# Fernhill Studio employee handbook

Fernhill Studio is an invented company. This handbook is synthetic sample data
written for this project. It contains no real policy and no personal data.

## Working hours

The standard working week is 37.5 hours, Monday to Friday. Core hours are 10:00
to 15:00, and everyone is expected to be reachable during core hours. Outside
core hours you may start and finish when it suits you, as long as your team
knows your schedule.

## Remote work

You may work from home up to three days a week. Tell your manager which days you
will be in the office by Friday of the week before. Working from another country
for more than ten working days in a year needs written approval from the people
team, because it can change where tax is paid.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees get 25 days of paid annual leave per year, plus public
holidays. Request leave in the people portal at least two weeks before the first
day you want off. You may carry over up to five unused days into the next year,
and carried-over days must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave

If you are ill, message your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.
You do not need a doctor's note for the first five working days of an illness.
From the sixth working day a doctor's note is required. Sick pay is paid at full
salary for up to four weeks in a year.

## Parental leave

Every new parent gets 16 weeks of parental leave at full salary, whether by
birth or by adoption. Tell the people team at least three months before the
expected start date. You may return part-time for the first eight weeks after
parental leave ends.

## Expenses

Submit expense claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase, with
a photo of the receipt. Claims above 500 euros need approval from your manager
before you spend the money. Approved claims are paid back with the next monthly
salary.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop on the first day. You may also spend up to 400
euros on home office equipment, such as a monitor or a chair, once every three
years. Report a lost or stolen laptop to the IT desk within 24 hours.

## Probation period

The probation period for new employees is three months. During probation the
notice period is one week for both sides. Your manager holds a review meeting
with you in the last two weeks of probation and confirms the result in writing.

## Training budget

Each employee has a training budget of 1,000 euros per year for courses, books,
and conferences. Ask your manager for approval before you book. An unused
training budget does not carry over into the next year.

## Dress code

There is no formal dress code in the office. Wear business clothes when you
visit a client or attend an external event.
