One idea runs through this whole roadmap: learn in a loop, not a straight line. You read, build, measure, and explain. Each step feeds the next, and the last one starts over. To measure, you open your demo in DevTools, the browser's built-in developer tools, and watch what actually happens.

Follow the four steps around the diagram, then put them back in order below.

<Diagram id="how-it-works" />

Reading the primary source gives you the real rules, not someone's summary. Building a small demo turns those rules into working code; MDN's learning-area repository holds runnable examples you can clone as a starter. Measuring checks you against a canonical self-check: MDN's "Test your skills" articles test one feature at a time, its Challenges test several together, and the MDN Playground runs each one in the browser. Explaining it in your own words finds the gap you missed, and that gap starts the next read.
