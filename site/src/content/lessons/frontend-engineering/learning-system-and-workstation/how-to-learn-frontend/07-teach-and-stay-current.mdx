Explain is the step most people skip, because the lesson feels finished once the code runs. Publishing on MDN makes it real: you write the page, open a pull request, and a reviewer corrects what you missed. To keep the read step fed, you subscribe to the Chrome for Developers release-notes feed, a stream of what each browser version shipped.

The diagram below is that publish-and-review loop. Step through it and name the step that catches your mistakes.

<Diagram id="mdn-publish-review-loop" />

The loop ends at "Page published", but the review is where you learn. A reviewer checks the writing, not just the code, and asks you to fix what you got wrong. That correction is the explain step doing its job.
