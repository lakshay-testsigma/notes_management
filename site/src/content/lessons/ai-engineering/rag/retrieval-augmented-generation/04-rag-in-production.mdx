Every part before the prompt is retrieval. A **retrieval failure** means the right chunk never reached the prompt. The post measures it as the share of relevant chunks missing from the top 20, and each fix in the diagram lowers that share: 5.7%, then 3.7%, 2.9%, and 1.9%. A **generation failure** means the chunk was there and the answer still went wrong, so no index change helps. The repair is in the prompt.

**Note.** These are Anthropic's own measurements, and they cover retrieval only, not the quality of the final answer. The same post says a knowledge base under about 200,000 tokens can go into the prompt whole, with no RAG at all.

**Fine-tuning**, which means retraining the model itself, repairs a different problem: use RAG when the model lacks knowledge or its knowledge is out of date, and fine-tuning when its behaviour is inconsistent or its format or tone is wrong.
