The retriever compares your question with pieces of text, not with whole documents. So before anything is embedded, the company handbook is cut into **chunks**: short pieces measured in tokens, the word pieces a model counts text in. How long should one piece be?

The curves pull in opposite directions. Large chunks fill the prompt with text the question never asked for. Small chunks keep it focused, but at 100 tokens the handbook is in 80 pieces, and a piece cut out of its section often no longer says what it is about. Anthropic's example is the chunk "The company's revenue grew by 3% over the previous quarter." It names no company and no quarter, so a question about ACME Corp in Q2 2023 may never find it.

**Overlap** repeats the last tokens of one chunk at the start of the next, so a sentence cut at a border stays whole in one of them. The price is more chunks to store.

**Note.** The curves are arithmetic, not measurements. The Anthropic post describes chunks of a few hundred tokens and uses 800 in its cost example.
